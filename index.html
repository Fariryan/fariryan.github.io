Fariryan

Research software

Interactive tools for neuroscience and drug discovery — built on public datasets, with provenance kept visible. Research and education only; not medical advice.

  • Drug Discovery Live

    Neurological diseases, therapeutics, molecular targets, chemical structures, pathways and clinical evidence, joined into one graph where every relationship carries its evidence level and source.

  • NeuroTumor3D Live

    Explainable brain MRI tumour classification for OpenNeuro ds006318, with Grad-CAM attention maps and rotatable pseudo-3D renderings. Research use only — not a medical device.

  • Bio-Model-3D Live

    A cinematic 3D atlas for exploring human and animal cell models in the browser.

  • Auditory fMRI Lab Not yet deployed

    Frequency cortical magnification in human auditory cortex at 7 Tesla, from OpenNeuro ds008460 — dataset retrieval, event-table conversion and browser-based inspection of participants, runs and NIfTI slices.

Source for each project lives in its own repository. Some applications are self-hosted and served through a Cloudflare Tunnel.
